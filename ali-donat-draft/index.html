Welcome.

I am a psychotherapist and executive coach in private practice. I provide a safe space for individuals to explore, often in a creative way, their internal worlds. If you are reading this, you might be looking to navigate difficult relationships, process past trauma, or simply find balance in a demanding professional life.

My background as a practising lawyer with extensive international experience has given me a deep understanding of the pressures faced by professionals leading busy lives, and how these demands can impact personal and emotional well-being.

  • UKCP Accredited Psychotherapist
  • BACP Member
  • International Coaching Federation: PCC
  • Columbia University Coaching Certification Program

My Approach to Therapy

Working integratively, I draw upon a variety of modalities to create a tailored approach that meets you exactly where you are.

Psychodynamic

Exploring how past experiences and unconscious patterns influence your present thoughts, behaviours, and relationships.

Existential & Intercultural

Understanding your unique worldview, exploring questions of meaning, and respecting the cultural contexts that shape your identity.

Person-Centred

Providing an empathetic, non-judgemental space where you are the expert of your own life, empowering your personal growth.
