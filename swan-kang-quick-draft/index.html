Frequently Asked Questions

What is the recovery time for blepharoplasty?
Initial swelling and bruising typically peak within the first 48 hours and resolve significantly by day 7-10 when your fine sutures are removed. Most patients feel comfortable returning to work, video calls, and social activities after 1-2 weeks.
Is eyelid lump removal painful?
No. Eyelid lump and chalazion removals are performed under local anaesthetic. You will feel a small sting from the initial injection, but the procedure itself is completely painless. It typically takes just 15-20 minutes in our minor ops suite.
What happens during the initial consultation?
Your initial consultation is a comprehensive appointment that includes a thorough medical history review, examination of your eye and facial anatomy, and a direct discussion with Miss Kang regarding your surgical options, expected outcomes, and a bespoke treatment plan tailored to your goals.
How long do botulinum toxin results last?
Results typically last between 3 to 4 months, depending on your individual metabolism and the specific area treated. Miss Kang focuses on natural movement and subtle enhancement, ensuring you look rested rather than artificial.
