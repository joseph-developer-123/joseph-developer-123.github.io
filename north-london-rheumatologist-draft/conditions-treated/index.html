Treatments & Services

Comprehensive rheumatology care tailored to your individual needs.

Examples of reasons people might come and see me:

Joint pain and/or swelling

Back pain

Raynaud's symptoms (cold hands that change colour)

Unexplained fatigue

Unexplained rashes, mouth ulcers, enlarged lymph nodes and fevers

Multisystem problems that need to be tied together

Hypermobility, palpitations, dizziness and widespread pain

Abnormal autoimmune/inflammatory blood results (eg ANA, ENA, ANCA, ESR, RF, CCP, HLA B27)

Low bone density (or risk of) and/or fractures

Abnormal imaging results suggesting joint or spine inflammation

Second opinion or an overview if management left unclear

I provide both in person and virtual reviews.

I offer help with the following services:

Expert diagnostic and therapeutic approaches focused on restoring your quality of life.

Comprehensive Diagnosis & Management Plan

Steroid joint and intramuscular injections

Fast access to diagnostic imaging

Second opinion in complex cases

Biologic & DMARD therapy

Referrals to physiotherapy & pain management
