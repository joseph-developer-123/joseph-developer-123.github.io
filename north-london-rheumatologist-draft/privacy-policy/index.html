Privacy Notice

Dr Anna Nuttall · Last updated: May 2026

Who we are

Dr Anna Nuttall is a specialist medical consultant practising in England and is the data controller for information collected through this website. Contact: info@northlondonrheumatologist.org

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry form, we collect the details you provide — typically your name, contact details, and a description of your medical query.

Why we collect it

Your information is used solely to respond to your consultation enquiry. Our lawful bases under UK GDPR are consent (Article 6(1)(a)) and, for health information, the provision of healthcare (Article 9(2)(h) and UK DPA 2018 Schedule 1).

How it's handled

Submissions are transmitted securely via Formspree, our email delivery processor, and received directly by Dr Nuttall. Formspree does not use your data for any other purpose. Their privacy policy is at formspree.io.

How long we keep it

Patient records are retained for a minimum of 8 years after last contact, in line with GMC guidance. Enquiries that do not lead to treatment are deleted within 12 months.

Who we share it with

Your data is not sold or shared for marketing purposes. It may be shared with healthcare professionals directly involved in your care, or where required by law.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict your data, to receive a portable copy, and to withdraw your consent at any time. If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you also have the right to complain to the ICO at ico.org.uk. To exercise any of these rights, email info@northlondonrheumatologist.org.
