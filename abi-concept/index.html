Biography

MBBS BSc MRCP PhD

Dr. Al-Hussaini is a Consultant Interventional and General Cardiologist with dual accreditation in General Medicine. She completed her medical studies at Imperial College London before undertaking prestigious interventional fellowships.

Her dedication to SCAD began when, as a final year Specialist Registrar, she treated a 32-year-old woman suffering from a post-partum SCAD. Witnessing the systemic lack of understanding and prolonged hospitalization this patient faced inspired Dr. Al-Hussaini to dedicate her career to this specialist niche.

Beyond clinical practice, she holds an MSc in Global Cardiovascular Health, Policy, Strategy, and Politics from the London School of Economics (LSE). She serves as an advisor on health in the Middle East and West Africa, actively working on healthcare reforms in low- and middle-income countries.

Patient Guide

Understanding Rare Coronary Diseases

Due to the rarity of SCAD and FMD, patients often undergo years of misdiagnosis. Education and health literacy are vital for long-term improvements in patient outcomes.

This authoritative guide helps patients understand the pathophysiology of these conditions and the importance of seeking an accredited specialist.

What is SCAD (Spontaneous Coronary Artery Dissection)? +

SCAD is a rare, emergency condition that occurs when a tear forms in a blood vessel in the heart. Unlike typical heart attacks caused by plaque buildup, SCAD is a structural tear. It disproportionately affects women, particularly those who are pregnant, post-partum, or experiencing hormonal shifts.

Because traditional heart disease risk factors (like high cholesterol or obesity) are often absent, patients presenting to A&E with SCAD are frequently misdiagnosed with anxiety. Dr. Al-Hussaini's specialised diagnostic protocol ensures rapid, accurate identification and tailored management.

How is Fibromuscular Dysplasia (FMD) linked to the heart? +

Fibromuscular Dysplasia (FMD) is a condition that causes narrowing (stenosis) and enlargement (aneurysm) of the medium-sized arteries in your body. There is a strong clinical correlation between FMD and SCAD. Patients diagnosed with SCAD should undergo comprehensive FMD screening.

Dr. Al-Hussaini utilizes advanced vascular imaging to detect FMD, ensuring a holistic view of the patient's arterial health to prevent future cardiovascular events.
