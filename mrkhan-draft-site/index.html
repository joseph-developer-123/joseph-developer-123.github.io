Conditions & Treatments

The latest eye tests and careful surgeries planned just for your daily life and vision needs.

Advanced Cataract Surgery

Our tiny-cut cataract surgery safely brings back your clear eyesight. We use top-quality artificial lenses made just for your lifestyle. By picking the right lens, we help you need glasses less often, or even not at all. You will see better quickly and recover very fast.

Glaucoma Management

Glaucoma needs careful, ongoing checks to save your eyesight. We use the latest scanners to spot tiny changes early on. Your personalised treatment plan might include modern laser therapies or gentle surgeries. These treatments help keep the pressure in your eye at a safe level with as little discomfort as possible.

Dry Eye Diagnostics

Long-lasting dry eye can be very painful, causing red, itchy eyes and blurry vision. We do special tests to find out exactly why your eyes are dry. Your personalised care plan might include special eye drops, tiny tear duct plugs, or eyelid treatments to help your eyes stay moist and comfortable every day.
