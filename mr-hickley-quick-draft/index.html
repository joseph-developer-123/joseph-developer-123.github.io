About The Surgeon

Dedicated to Enhancing Your Vision & Wellbeing

Welcome. I am Mr. Nicholas Hickley, a Consultant Ophthalmic and Oculoplastic Surgeon. My approach is simple: I treat every patient with the empathy, transparency, and clinical excellence they deserve. Whether you are seeking vision restoration through modern cataract surgery or complex periorbital reconstruction, you are in safe, experienced hands.

My clinic utilizes state-of-the-art diagnostic technology combined with proven, minimally invasive surgical techniques to ensure rapid recovery and outstanding aesthetic and visual outcomes. We focus on you as an individual, tailoring every treatment plan to fit your specific lifestyle needs.
