About Miss Kang

Miss Swan Kang is a distinguished, board-certified oculoplastic surgeon based in central London. With advanced fellowship training and an extensive background in both functional and aesthetic eyelid surgery, she brings unparalleled precision to her practice.

Combining rigorous anatomical knowledge with an acute eye for aesthetic harmony, Miss Kang is renowned for delivering results that are both transformative and inherently natural. She takes a highly bespoke approach, ensuring every procedure is meticulously tailored to the individual.

Her commitment to excellence extends from the first consultation through to comprehensive aftercare, fostering a trusted environment where patient wellbeing and superior surgical outcomes are paramount.
