UPPER
BLEPHAROPLASTY

Restoring Clarity & Vitality

The upper eyelids are a sophisticated architectural element of the face, serving both to protect the globe and ensure the healthy distribution of the tear film. Over time, however, these delicate tissues are uniquely susceptible to the cumulative effects of gravity, sun exposure, and the constant mechanical motion of blinking - occurring thousands of times each day.

By the time we reach our 60s, the skin often loses its structural elasticity, leading to dermatochalasis, or "hooding." This excess tissue can rest heavily on the lashes, creating a tired appearance and, in many cases, physically obstructing the upper field of vision.

An upper blepharoplasty is a precise surgical intervention designed to address these changes. By meticulously removing redundant skin and, where necessary, repositioning underlying fat, I aim to restore a refreshed, open-eyed contour that feels lighter and looks entirely natural.

Whether driven by functional necessity or a desire for aesthetic rejuvenation, the goal remains the same: a balanced outcome that respects your unique anatomy. A detailed consultation is essential to tailor the surgical plan to your specific goals and eyelid health.

FAQS

What is the recovery time like?

Most patients can return to normal activities and work within 7 to 10 days. Mild swelling and bruising are normal but subside significantly within the first two weeks.

Will I have visible scars?

Incisions are meticulously placed within the natural creases of your eyelids, rendering them virtually undetectable once fully healed.
