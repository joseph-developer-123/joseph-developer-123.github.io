.01 About Me

My Approach

As a BPC accredited and BAPC registered psychotherapist in Central London, I offer a warm, insightful, and collaborative space. Together, we can explore the patterns shaping your relationships and begin shifting what no longer serves you. Drawing on extensive experience, I bring clarity and steadiness to both times of crisis and the work of lasting change.

Seeking a private therapist in London takes courage, especially when things feel unclear. I work with successful professionals, individuals navigating life transitions, and those feeling disconnected or at odds with themselves.

Across my work in the NHS, private settings (including The Priory), and private practice, I have supported clients through complex difficulties including anxiety, depression, trauma, and addiction counselling. The work unfolds at a pace that feels right for you.

Qualifications & Registrations

  • I'm a fully registered member of the British Association of Counsellors and Psychotherapists, the British Psychoanalytic Council, and Addiction Professionals.
  • I hold an MSc in Psychodynamic Counselling and Psychotherapy from Birkbeck College, University of London, and further qualifications from CCPE and Tavistock Relationships.
  • I have completed additional trainings in Mentalization Based Treatment, Group Dynamics, and Trauma-Focussed CBT.
.02 Services & Approach

Bringing things back into focus.

Therapy offers a powerful opportunity to discover who you really are and untangle the parts of yourself that challenge or confuse you the most.

The Space

A collaborative, safe environment grounded in empathy. We take account of your real-world experiences, including the impact of trauma, identity, and cultural background.

The Work

Deep and impactful. We use the relationship that develops between us to gain insight into unconscious patterns, leading to profound self-awareness.

The Start

A complimentary phone call to understand your goals, followed by a 50-minute exploratory session to ensure we are the right fit for your journey.

What I Can Help With

I specialise in providing in-person and online therapy in London for those facing complex mental health matters. Whether you require addiction therapy in Central London, trauma counselling, or support for high-functioning anxiety, I am here to help you rebuild and recover.
