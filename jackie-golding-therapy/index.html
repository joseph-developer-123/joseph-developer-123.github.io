Navigating your way through a difficult time can feel daunting on your own.

Maybe it’s a difficult relationship, a stressful work life, painful family dynamics, or feeling overwhelmed by past events. Whatever you’re struggling with, therapy is a place to be fully heard.

My path began many years ago in the corporate world, so I relate to the stresses of working life. After completing intensive postgraduate training, I now run a practice helping people integrate the challenges they face on their journey.

Having had many years of personal psychotherapy myself, I fully appreciate how it feels to be a client stepping into the consulting room for the first time.
