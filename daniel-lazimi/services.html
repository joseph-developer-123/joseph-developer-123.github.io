My Services

Where connection is more than a goal...

Collaborative, person-centred therapy designed around what matters most to you. We look at where life may feel chaotic or overwhelming, and where values, structure, and conscious choice might restore a sense of orientation.

Who This Is Ideal For

I work with adults, older adults, trainees, and young people dealing with:

  • ✦ Anxiety, depression, and low mood
  • ✦ Trauma and emotional regulation difficulties
  • ✦ Neurodivergence (ADD/ADHD)
  • ✦ Self-harm, high-risk behaviours, and addictions
  • ✦ Loss, bereavement, and life transitions
01

Trauma & Regulation

Working at a pace that supports emotional safety. We explore how history influences your present, helping to process what you carry.

02

Neurodiversity (ADHD)

Neurodiversity-affirming therapy. Together we explore identity, structure, and choice to help restore orientation and agency.

03

Anxiety & Depression

For when life feels stuck. We use an integrative approach to notice patterns, explore meaning, and shape an authentic life.

04

Addiction & Recovery

Providing a non-judgemental space to understand dependency, high-risk behaviours, and the path to recovery.

05

Identity & Relationships

Exploring self-esteem, existential questions, and interpersonal difficulties during times of uncertainty or transition.
