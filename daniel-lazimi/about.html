Meet Daniel

Daniel Lazimi

Registered Member MBACP | Integrative Therapist & Mental Health Nurse

📍 Manchester & Online 🗣️ English, Hebrew

My Background: Alongside my counselling training, I have six years’ experience as a registered mental health nurse. This background supports me to work thoughtfully with complexity, risk, and safeguarding, while remaining grounded in ethical, relational practice.

My experience includes work in a private substance misuse and rehabilitation clinic, supporting students with neurodiversity and disabilities in higher education, and working as a school counsellor in a specialist SEN setting.

Our Philosophy

I aim to offer a space where you can reflect on your experiences, notice patterns, and consider how your history, current circumstances, and sense of direction influence the way you live your life.

I work integratively with a person-centred foundation, informed by existential, trauma-informed, and neurodiversity-affirming perspectives to build a respectful, containing therapeutic relationship.
