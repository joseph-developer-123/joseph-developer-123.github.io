ABOUT JACKIE

My path began many years ago in the corporate world, so I can relate to the stresses that come with working life. After feeling unfulfilled for some time, I felt the pull towards a vocation where I could be in service to others. After completing intensive training at postgraduate level, I now run a therapy practice helping people explore and integrate the challenges they are dealing with on their own journey.

I have a particular interest in helping with relationship issues whether they are troubled, broken, or abusive. Having worked with a diverse range of people, I also specialize in childhood trauma, PTSD, anxiety, depression, ADHD, addiction, low self-esteem, shame, and women's issues including menopause.

MY APPROACH

Psychosynthesis

Working together to explore and integrate the varied challenges you are dealing with on your unique personal journey.

Transactional Analysis

Understanding the limiting beliefs and deep-seated, destructive patterns to foster healthier relationships with yourself and others.

Psychodynamic Focus

Providing a safe space to recognize how early life events, childhood trauma, and past impacts shape present relational difficulties.

Transpersonal & Spiritual

Embracing personal growth, the search for meaning, existential exploration, and making sense of profound life experiences.

Humanistic Care

A place of unconditional acceptance and genuine positive regard, allowing you to explore your authentic self without judgement.

Mindfulness & Presence

Holding a compassionate, grounded space with my full presence, ensuring we work at a pace that is comfortable and validating for you.
