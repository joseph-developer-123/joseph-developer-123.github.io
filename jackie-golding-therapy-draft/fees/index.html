FEES & AVAILABILITY

Availability

On Mondays, Wednesdays and Thursdays I offer therapy online or face to face in Tonbridge (mornings & evenings).

On Tuesdays I work in person at London Bridge (afternoon & evening).

Fees

My fee is £60 for in person sessions and £50 online.

Each session is 50 minutes and can be weekly or fortnightly.

I believe that counselling should be available for everyone so I may have low-cost places available - please ask if you are on low income.

To find out more please message me and we can arrange a free, no obligation chat.
