Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about booking, fees, tests and your appointment.

This service is not for emergencies. If you have severe chest pain, sudden breathlessness, or you or someone else has collapsed, call 999 immediately.

Booking and fees

Do I need a GP referral?

If you are self-funding, you can book directly. A GP referral letter is helpful for understanding your background but is not required for an initial appointment.

If you are using private medical insurance, you will typically need a GP referral letter and an authorisation code from your insurer.

Do you see patients with private medical insurance?

Yes, Dr Aung is recognised by most major insurers. Please check your policy with your insurer before booking. Dr Aung does not currently see patients insured with Bupa.

How much does a consultation cost?

Fees can be confirmed by the practice secretary or during the online booking process. See the Fees & Insurance page for more information.

How do I book an appointment?

You can book online through the secure patient portal, or call or email the practice secretary on +44 (0)7356 015504 / info@drnayaung.co.uk. If you cannot find a suitable time online, the secretary can help.

Your appointment

Who can be seen?

Dr Aung sees adults aged 18 and over.

What can Dr Aung help with?

Chest pain or tightness, palpitations, breathlessness, dizziness and blackouts, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, heart health checks, family history of heart disease, and known conditions such as heart failure, valve disease, cardiomyopathy and coronary artery disease. See the Conditions page for more detail.

What happens at my first appointment?

Dr Aung will listen to your symptoms and history, explain what he finds in plain language, and arrange further tests if they are needed. The aim is that you leave knowing what is going on and what happens next.

It helps to bring your GP letter (if you have one), a list of your medicines, and any previous heart tests or scan reports.

Can I get a second opinion?

Yes. Dr Aung offers a fresh review of a diagnosis, scan result or treatment plan. Please bring any reports or images you have.

Tests

Which tests are available?

ECG, echocardiogram (heart ultrasound), Holter monitoring, 24-hour blood pressure monitoring, exercise testing, cardiac MRI and CT coronary angiography. See the Tests page for what each involves.

Who reports my scans?

Dr Aung personally reports cardiac MRI and echocardiography, so your scan is interpreted by the cardiologist who knows your case.

Where and who

Where is the clinic?

Private appointments are at Welbeck Heart Health, 1 Welbeck Street, London W1G 0AR, in the Harley Street Medical District.

Can I be seen on the NHS?

Dr Aung is also a consultant at Barts Heart Centre, St Bartholomew's Hospital. NHS appointments there are by referral only.

How is my personal information used?

Your details are processed by the practice to arrange and manage your care. See the privacy policy for details.

Where I practise

Private appointments at Welbeck in the Harley Street Medical District. NHS appointments at Barts Heart Centre are by referral only.
