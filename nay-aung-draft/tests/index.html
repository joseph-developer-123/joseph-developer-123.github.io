Diagnostic Tests

Precise diagnostic evaluations, personally reported by Dr Nay Aung to ensure continuity and accuracy[cite: 2].

ECG (Electrocardiogram)

A quick test that records the electrical activity of your heart, helping to identify arrhythmias, electrical conduction disturbances, and signs of previous heart strain[cite: 2].

Echocardiogram (Heart Ultrasound)

An ultrasound scan providing detailed moving images of your heart's valves, chambers, and pumping function, personally interpreted by Dr Aung[cite: 2].

Holter Monitoring (Heart Rhythm Recording)

Continuous monitoring of your heart rhythm over 24 hours or longer to capture intermittent palpitations, skipped beats, or underlying arrhythmias like atrial fibrillation[cite: 2].

24-Hour Blood Pressure Monitoring

Portable tracking of your blood pressure over a full day and night cycle to diagnose hypertension or assess control accuracy free from 'white coat' effects[cite: 2].

Exercise Tolerance Test

Monitoring your heart's performance and electrical response while walking on a treadmill to investigate exertional chest discomfort or exercise capacity[cite: 2].

Cardiac MRI

Advanced, non-invasive cross-sectional imaging providing exquisite tissue characterisation and chamber volumes, backed by Dr Aung's EACVI Level 3 certification[cite: 2].

CT Coronary Angiography

Advanced CT imaging used to visualise the coronary arteries non-invasively, assessing for plaque buildup and ruling out coronary artery disease[cite: 2].
