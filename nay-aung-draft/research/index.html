2026 - iHeF - AI Heart Failure Screening from a Routine ECG

I lead the development of iHeF, an AI tool that screens for heart failure from a standard ECG. In 2026 the project received £1.3m from the National Institute for Health and Care Research to prepare it for NHS use.

Queen Mary press release (11 June 2026) · Barts Health news · Queen Mary Innovation project page · EurekAlert · Medical Update Online

2026 - Muscle, Fat and Heart Risk - The Sarcopenic Obesity Index (JACC)

My research suggests why muscle and fat may tell us more about heart risk than BMI. Published as senior author in JACC.

2024 - Inherited Cardiac Amyloidosis (JAMA Cardiology)

As first author, I published research in JAMA Cardiology on inherited cardiac amyloidosis, a condition in which abnormal protein builds up in the heart muscle. The study found that more people may be at risk of this hereditary heart disease than previously thought.

Queen Mary press release

2023 - Telomere Length and Heart Failure (JAMA Cardiology)

As first author, I published research in JAMA Cardiology linking telomere length, a marker of biological ageing, to heart function, heart size and the risk of heart failure.

Medscape · Physicians Weekly

2023 - Expert Consensus on Excessive Trabeculation

I co-authored an expert consensus on excessive trabeculation of the heart muscle, which called for a refreshed look at the clinical approach to this heart muscle disorder. I was quoted in the coverage.

Newswise

2022 - Genetics of the Right Ventricle (Nature Genetics)

As first author, I published research in Nature Genetics on the genetics of the right ventricle, the heart chamber that pumps blood to the lungs.

William Harvey Research Institute news (14 June 2022)

2020 and 2017 - Cholesterol and the Heart (JACC and EuroCMR)

My research suggests that cholesterol affects the heart muscle itself, not only the arteries. In over 17,000 UK Biobank participants, people genetically predisposed to higher LDL (“bad”) cholesterol and triglycerides had changes in the structure of the heart's main pumping chamber (JACC, 2020). Earlier, I found that people taking statins were less likely to have a thickened or enlarged heart (presented at EuroCMR 2017).

Aung N, et al. The Effect of Blood Lipids on the Left Ventricle - A Mendelian Randomization Study. J Am Coll Cardiol 2020;76:2477-2488.

JACC paper (PubMed) · JACC editorial · European Society of Cardiology press release · ScienceDaily · The Pharmaceutical Journal · Cardiac Interventions Today (2 June 2017)

2020 - Obesity, Cholesterol and COVID-19 Risk (Frontiers in Genetics)

As lead author, I used genetic data to investigate whether obesity and related cardiometabolic factors, such as cholesterol, may influence susceptibility to COVID-19.

Queen Mary release · EurekAlert

2019 - Cannabis and Heart Structure (JACC Cardiovascular Imaging, co-author)

I co-authored research that identified a possible link between cannabis use and structural changes to the heart.

Queen Mary news

2018 - Air Pollution and Heart Structure (Circulation)

My research using cardiac MRI scans from nearly 4,000 UK Biobank participants found that people with higher long-term exposure to traffic-related air pollution had larger heart chambers, a change also seen in the early stages of heart failure.
