2026: iHeF — AI Heart Failure Screening from a Routine ECG

I lead the development of iHeF, an AI tool that screens for heart failure from a standard ECG. In 2026 the project received £1.3m from the National Institute for Health and Care Research to prepare it for NHS use.

Queen Mary Press Release · Barts Health News · QM Innovation Project Page

2026: Muscle, Fat and Heart Risk — The Sarcopenic Obesity Index (JACC)

My research suggests why muscle and fat may tell us more about heart risk than BMI. Published as senior author in JACC.

2026: Air Pollution Research Cited in Parliament Evidence

Cited by the British Heart Foundation in written evidence to the House of Commons Environmental Audit Committee (2026), referencing my 2018 Circulation study.

Read BHF Written Evidence

2018: Air Pollution and Heart Structure (Circulation)

My research using cardiac MRI scans from nearly 4,000 UK Biobank participants found that people with higher long-term exposure to traffic-related air pollution had larger heart chambers, a change also seen in the early stages of heart failure.
