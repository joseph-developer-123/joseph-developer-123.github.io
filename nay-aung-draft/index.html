Specialist assessment and treatment for adults, with detailed tests when they're needed.

Special interests

Alongside general cardiology, I have specialist expertise in cardiac imaging and heart failure. I personally report cardiac MRI and echocardiography, so your scan is interpreted by the cardiologist who knows your case. These are also the focus of my research at Queen Mary University of London.

Research that shapes my practice

My research uses cardiac MRI, genetics and artificial intelligence to find heart disease earlier. It has been published in over 150 peer-reviewed papers, in journals including Nature Genetics and JACC, and featured by BBC News and the Guardian.

In the news: air pollution and the heart

My research using cardiac MRI scans from nearly 4,000 UK Biobank participants found that people with higher long-term exposure to traffic-related air pollution had larger heart chambers, a change also seen in the early stages of heart failure. The study was published in Circulation and covered by BBC News, the Guardian, The Times and the Telegraph.

Detecting heart failure from a routine ECG

I lead the development of iHeF, an AI tool that screens for heart failure from a standard ECG. In 2026 the project received £1.3m from the National Institute for Health and Care Research to prepare it for NHS use.
