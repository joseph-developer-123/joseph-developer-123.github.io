Research that shapes my practice

Over 150 peer-reviewed papers using cardiac MRI, genetics and AI to find heart disease earlier.

Air pollution and the heart

Cardiac MRI scans from nearly 4,000 UK Biobank participants showed larger heart chambers in people with higher long-term exposure to traffic-related pollution, a change also seen in early heart failure. Published in Circulation.

Read the studyBBC News

Detecting heart failure from a routine ECG

I lead iHeF, an AI tool that screens for heart failure from a standard ECG. In 2026 the project received £1.3m from the NIHR to prepare it for NHS use.
