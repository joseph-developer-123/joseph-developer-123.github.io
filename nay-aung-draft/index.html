Dr Nay Aung

Consultant Cardiologist at Welbeck Cardiology Clinic, London and Barts Health Centre, London. Providing continuous clarity for complex symptoms. Combining state-of-the-art computational Cardiac MRI and genetic metrics to diagnose heart anomalies.

Arrange Private Appointment Explore Diagnostic Capabilities

"Most people come to see me because something doesn't feel right with their heart. Breathlessness, palpitations, chest discomfort, or dizziness that's never been properly explained. I use the most detailed cardiac imaging available, including cardiac MRI, to work out exactly what's causing your symptoms, rather than a process of ruling things out one at a time. Seeing me means getting a precise diagnosis, explained clearly, and a treatment plan built around what's actually happening in your heart."

— DR NAY AUNG

Comprehensive Diagnostic Mastery

Targeted medical strategies utilising deep phenotypic analysis, computational data science frameworks, and advanced diagnostic protocols.

Heart Failure Management

Advanced diagnostic algorithms, multimodal staging assessments, and optimisation therapeutics to target and reverse chronic myocardial stress trajectories.

Advanced Cardiac MRI

EACVI Level-3-certified complex imaging. Tissue characterisation, stress perfusion sequences, T1/T2 mapped profiling, and radiomics.

Genetic Cardiomyopathy

In-depth management of inherited conditions and whole-genome/exome array interpretations to identify early phenotypic inheritance.

Preventive Cardiology

Executive health matrix tracking lipid sub-fractions, cardiovascular risk scores, and ambient environmental stressors alongside lifestyle configurations.

Coronary Artery Disease

Utilising ultra-precise CT coronary angiography, calcium scoring maps, and functional stress parameters to evaluate macrovascular ischaemia.

Atrial Fibrillation & Arrhythmias

Comprehensive investigation and rhythmic analysis mapping unexplained palpitations, structural load abnormalities, or recurrent syncopal events.
