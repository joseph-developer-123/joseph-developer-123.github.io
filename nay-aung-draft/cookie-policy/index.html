Cookie Policy

How this website uses cookies and third-party services.

Last updated 23 October 2026

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files that a website can store on your device. They are often used to remember your settings, keep you signed in, or measure how a site is used.

Cookies on this website

This website does not set any cookies of its own. It does not use analytics, advertising or social media tracking cookies. Because of this, we do not show a cookie banner.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts. The fonts used on this website are loaded from Google's servers. When your browser requests them, Google receives your IP address and basic browser details. Google states that the Google Fonts service does not set or log cookies.
  • Carebit patient portal. The “Book an appointment” buttons take you to the Carebit patient portal, which is a separate service. It may set cookies that are needed to sign you in and keep your session secure. Please see Carebit's own policies for details.

Managing cookies

You can block or delete cookies through your browser settings. Guidance is available from the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk. Blocking cookies may stop the patient portal from working properly.

Changes to this policy

If we add analytics or other non-essential cookies in future, we will update this page and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.
