Fees & Insurance

Transparent pricing and health insurance information for private appointments at Welbeck Heart Health.

Consultation Fees

Dr Nay Aung sees self-funding patients and patients with major medical insurance providers. Specific consultation and testing fees can be confirmed directly via the secretary or during the online booking process through Carebit.

Insured Patients

Dr Nay Aung is recognised by most major insurance providers. Please check your policy details with your insurer before booking your appointment. You will typically require a GP referral letter and a valid pre-authorisation code from your insurance provider. Note: Dr Nay Aung does not accept Bupa patients at this time.

Self-Funding Patients

Self-funding patients are welcome to book directly. While a GP referral letter is helpful to understand your clinical background, it is not strictly mandatory for initial self-funding appointments.

Private Healthcare Information Network (PHIN)

In compliance with the Private Healthcare Market Investigation Order, fees, insurer recognitions, and patient feedback statistics are made publicly available. You can view Dr Nay Aung’s official profile on the Private Healthcare Information Network website.

Where I practise

Private appointments at Welbeck in the Harley Street Medical District. NHS appointments at Barts Heart Centre are by referral only.
