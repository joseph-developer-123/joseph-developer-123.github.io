Conditions & Symptoms

Specialist assessment and treatment for adult heart conditions, with advanced diagnostics when needed.

Symptoms

Chest pain or tightness

Finding out whether it's your heart, and what to do about it.

Palpitations and irregular heartbeat

Assessment of racing, skipping or irregular heartbeats, including atrial fibrillation.

Breathlessness and tiredness

Checking whether your heart is the cause.

Dizziness and blackouts

Investigating fainting and light-headedness.

Risk and Prevention

High blood pressure

Including blood pressure that's hard to control.

High cholesterol

Understanding your numbers and your options.

Heart health checks

A personalised assessment of your risk of heart disease.

Family history of heart disease

Screening and advice if a relative has had heart problems at a young age.

Known Heart Conditions

Heart failure

Finding the cause of a weakened or stiff heart, and building a treatment plan to protect and improve it.

Heart valve disease

Assessment and monitoring of leaking or narrowed valves.

Cardiomyopathy

Assessment and management of heart muscle disease.

Coronary artery disease

Investigating chest pain and coronary risk, using CT coronary angiography or stress imaging where appropriate.

Second opinions

A fresh review of a diagnosis, scan result or treatment plan.
