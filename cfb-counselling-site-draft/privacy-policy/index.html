Privacy Policy

How your personal data is collected, used, and securely stored.

This Privacy Policy describes how Claire Frances ("I", "me", "my") collects, uses, and securely stores the personal data you provide when using clairefrancescounselling@gmail.com or contacting me regarding integrative counselling services.

1. Data Controller

I am the Data Controller for the personal information you provide. I am a registered therapist, and I adhere to professional ethical frameworks alongside the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR).

2. Information I Collect

When you use the contact form on this website, I collect the following information:

  • Identity Data: Your name.
  • Contact Data: Your email address and phone number.
  • Health Data: Any information you voluntarily share regarding your mental health or reasons for seeking counselling within the "Message" field of the contact form.

3. How I Use Your Information

I process your data under the following legal bases:

  • Consent: When you submit an enquiry, you consent to me using your details to respond to you.
  • Performance of a Contract: If we agree to work together, processing is necessary to provide your therapy sessions.
  • Legitimate Interests: To manage my professional practice safely and effectively.

4. Data Retention

If you contact me but we do not proceed with therapy, I will delete your enquiry after 6 months. If you become a client, I am required by my professional insurance to keep clinical records for 7 years after our work concludes, after which they are securely destroyed.

5. Your Rights

Under the UK GDPR, you have the right to access the data I hold about you, request corrections, or request erasure of your data. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO) if you have concerns about how your data is handled.
