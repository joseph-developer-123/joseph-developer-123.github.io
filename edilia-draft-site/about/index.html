My Story

I am Edília, a CBT Therapist and Chartered Psychologist integrating clinical practice, forensic psychology, and social justice. I believe psychological distress is inseparable from the systems and structures shaping our daily lives.

I specialise in trauma from domestic abuse, migration, and inequality. I bring this same non-judgmental, compassionate care to everyone I work with, including men.

My background studying violence and systemic disadvantage in forensic contexts means I understand complexity—and I know the immense courage it takes to ask for help.

My Approach

I work using Cognitive Behavioural Therapy (CBT) within a trauma-informed framework. This means we work with your thoughts, feelings, and behaviours - but always with an awareness of the broader context that has shaped your experiences. Therapy with me is collaborative, honest, and grounded in respect for your pace.

I do not believe in a one-size-fits-all approach. Many of the people I work with have faced not only personal pain but systemic barriers - discrimination, displacement, injustice. I work to create a space that feels genuinely safe, culturally aware, and free from judgment.

Sessions are delivered online, making therapy accessible wherever you are - across the UK and internationally, including for Portuguese-speaking clients worldwide.

"I bring forensic psychology, clinical expertise, and a deep understanding of the social forces that shape human suffering - because people deserve more than surface-level support."

Who I Work With

I work with adults (18+) online, across the UK and internationally. My clients come from many different backgrounds, cultures, and life stages — what they share is a desire to understand themselves more deeply and move forward.

I work with people experiencing depression, anxiety, and complex emotional difficulties, particularly those connected to domestic abuse, discrimination, migration, and relational trauma. I also work with men, and welcome clients navigating identity, cultural adjustment, and the aftermath of difficult or unjust systems.

For Portuguese-speaking clients based in the UK, Europe, Africa, or elsewhere — I offer therapy and consultancy in Portuguese, in a space that understands your cultural context.
