A Safe Space for Deep Healing

I provide trauma-informed psychological support tailored for individuals experiencing depression, anxiety, and complex emotional difficulties. My practice is fundamentally built on understanding the profound impacts of discrimination, migration, and systemic marginalisation.

Therapy should not feel cold or purely clinical. It is a collaborative relationship where we address both your individual experiences and the broader societal contexts that shape them, working together towards lasting empowerment.
