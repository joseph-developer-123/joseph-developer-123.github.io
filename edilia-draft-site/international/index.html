International Consultancy, Training & Research

I support global organisations, international NGOs, public services, community initiatives, educational institutions, and cross-border programs in developing trauma-informed, culturally responsive, and evidence-based approaches to practice.

My areas of expertise encompass navigating complex systemic challenges, including gender-based violence, domestic abuse, migration-related trauma, social inclusion, and forensic psychological presentations.

Consultancy services are tailored to the distinct cultural, linguistic, and operational realities of each country or international development framework, encompassing service design and policy implementation.

Global Training & Workshops

I provide specialized professional training, capacity-building workshops, and interactive webinars for international organisations, practitioners, community services, and multidisciplinary teams.

Key topics focus heavily on understanding trauma in migration contexts, cross-cultural competence, trauma-informed criminal justice practices, and preventing burnout within high-pressure international aid environments.

Training can be delivered seamlessly online or in person, adapted to your local regional context and specific linguistic needs.
