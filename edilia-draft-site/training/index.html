Consultancy, Training & Research

I support organisations, NGOs, public services, community projects, educational institutions, and international initiatives in developing trauma-informed, culturally responsive, and evidence-based approaches to practice.

My areas of expertise include gender-based violence, domestic abuse, trauma-informed services, mental health, youth violence prevention, and criminal justice responses.

Consultancy services can be tailored to the specific needs, objectives, and context of each organisation, including service development, policy implementation, and programme design.

Training & Workshops

I provide professional training and workshops for organisations, practitioners, community services, educational institutions, and multidisciplinary teams.

Key topics encompass understanding trauma, trauma-informed responses to gender-based violence, stress and burnout prevention, and cultural competence.

Training can be delivered online or in person and adapted to the specific needs of organisations and teams.
