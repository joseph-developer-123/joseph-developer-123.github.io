Academic Leadership

Continually advancing the global standard of cardiac care.

Here I spoke with Adam Pick - heart valve patient advocate, author, and founder of HeartValveSurgery.com - about how heart valve disease is under-diagnosed and undertreated. But when caught early and managed at the right centre, it can be curative. My latest publication in The Lancet makes the case for wider care networks, earlier intervention, and modern endoscopic techniques.

01

Lifetime Management of Heart Valve Disease

The Lancet • Co-Authored Paper
2024
Abstract: This paper emphasises early intervention in heart valve disease. When treated early and right the first time, heart valve disease can be curative. We advocate for patients to seek specialist networks that offer advanced endoscopic techniques to minimize physical trauma and optimize long-term survivability.
