Endoscopic Mitral Valve Surgery

An overview of minimally invasive heart valve procedures, clinical outcomes, and patient suitability under the care of Consultant Cardiothoracic Surgeon Mr Joseph Zacharias.

The Clinical Approach

Compared to traditional open surgery, an endoscopic approach accesses the heart through small incisions between the ribs. Documented clinical data demonstrates clear physiological benefits for patient recovery, including:

  • Shorter inpatient hospital stay
  • Quantifiably reduced intra-operative blood loss
  • Lower statistical risk of post-operative infection
  • Accelerated return to baseline activities
  • Avoidance of median sternotomy

This keyhole methodology aligns fully with NICE guidelines for managing structural heart valve disease in anatomically suitable patients.

Procedural Overview & Surgeon Experience

Avoiding the structural trauma of a median sternotomy through specialised instrumentation.

Standard mitral surgery generally requires a full vertical division of the sternum to access the thoracic cavity. Endoscopic surgery bypasses this entirely. The procedure utilises specialised long-shafted instruments and a high-definition thoracoscopic camera inserted through a lateral mini-thoracotomy.


Mr Zacharias completed his advanced fellowships under European pioneers in Belgium in 2005, subsequently adapting strict protocols to introduce and establish a high-volume endoscopic programme in the UK. His surgical team continues to contribute regularly to clinical research and national randomised controlled trials to evaluate and advance minimally invasive outcomes.

Patient Assessment and Suitability

All patients can be considered for the minimally invasive ART approach for aortic valve replacement.


As part of the comprehensive assessment process, a CT scan is completed to ascertain whether there is a clear arterial tree. This is a critical anatomical check, as an artery in the groin is used for the bypass machine during the operation to ensure patient safety and procedural success.
